How PrivLab gets smarter

Here's the simple version: PrivLab doesn't change any AI. It just remembers what you show it, right here on your computer, and uses it on your next report.

When you hide personal details

Hide it once. Say you hide a name. From then on, PrivLab hides it for you in every new report, before anything goes anywhere.

Not personal? Keep it. If you leave a highlighted word visible, say "Bloom" in "Bloom-Richardson grade", PrivLab won't ask about it next time.

One exception. A single word like "Tan" could be a name or a colour, so PrivLab only skips it in the report you're on and checks again next time.

It stays with you. All of this is kept on your computer, encrypted.

When you fill in your fields

Your answers become examples. Every time you confirm or fix a value, PrivLab keeps it as an example. Next time, the AI sees your examples (never names or ID numbers), so it picks values the way you would.

Only your checks count. Values saved by Extract all, or ones you reset, don't teach it.

It earns your trust. A field only gets the Ready badge after about 60 checks with no mistakes. Now and then PrivLab hides its answer and asks you first, so you're never just nodding along.

Changed your mind about something? You can see and undo all of it in Fields → Learned.
